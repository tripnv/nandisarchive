When do we get there

2026-01-15 00:03

I feel like echo chambers are becoming increasingly loud. And, tech is inherently one such chamber.

These days the future of programming stands trial, and the machine is at its forefront. Skeptics still dismiss it as a fad, accelerationists overlook its faults, infinte threads, infinite reels...

At first, it was a shortcut for syntax related questions; then explaining code snippets, disentangling regex patterns, writing boilerplate, later the invaluable debugging tool. At some point, we start defining, prototyping workflows, end-to-end agentic processes. I write less and less. There are setbacks of course, but we progress. In the moment, we often feel stuck, but as days, weeks and months pass, it becomes evident that there won't be much left for me to write. And I don't mind.

Within a year, we go through deepseek, o3, sonnet, opus, 3.5, 4.0, 4.5; and the handheld programming experience transforms into a production line. Responsability and accountability stay with us, but we offload more and more implementation details onto our sidekicks. After all the system is what matters.

Abstraction at its finest, one could say.
